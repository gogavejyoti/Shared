Executive takeaway

The most important non-obvious finding is that the contact center does not primarily have an average headcount problem. It has an interval alignment problem. A positive average staffing gap coexists with poor service and high ASA in localized periods. That points to schedule placement, forecast timing, skill mix, breaks, adherence, or intraday response as the first areas to investigate.

Before pursuing broad automation or permanent FTE changes, leadership should validate the Monday risk pattern, quantify schedule adherence and shrinkage, and join queue/skill-level demand to agent-state data.
